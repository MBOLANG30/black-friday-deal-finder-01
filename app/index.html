How the prices were checked

Every deal on this page was checked on November 27, 2026 (UTC), on the store's own product page or in a deals source that names the store — the source and check time are printed on every deal. The “was” price is the store's list or comparable value. Prices change fast in Black Friday week, so confirm the live price at the store before you buy.

The verdict on each deal is honest, not flattering: NEW LOW means a record-low price the source says so about; STRONG DEAL is a large, real cut; ROUTINE DEAL is a real but small or common cut; INFLATED LIST means the price itself is good but the “was” price is pumped up, so the percentage overstates the saving.
